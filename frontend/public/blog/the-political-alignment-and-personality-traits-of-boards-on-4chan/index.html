The political alignment and personality traits of boards on 4chan

Published May 6, 2024
Updated May 6, 2024

As we found out in a previous blog post, men and women on 4chan differ substantially in their political views, with women being markedly more liberal than their male counterparts. In this blog post, we take a look at the politics of 4channers by the boards they browse.

While 4chan is reputed to be a right-wing, Internet hate machine (mostly thanks to /pol/), there's also boards which hold predominantly opposing views or are apolitical, like /lgbt/ ("Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, & Transgender" people) and /out/ ("Outdoors"). As we'll see, these boards often not only have contrasting political views but temperaments and habits too.

Who's in the cohort?

We used Duolicious clubs to determine which user belonged to which board. Duolicious users can join zero or more clubs. Joining a club in Duolicious causes mutual members to be prioritized when searching for matches.

Because most Duolicious users are currently from 4chan, lots of clubs on Duolicious correspond to 4chan boards. The most popular clubs on Duolicious are /v/ (Video Games), /pol/ (Politically Incorrect), and /a/ (Anime & Manga). But there's other clubs too, such as gamer, Anime, goth, philosophy, and so on.

The clubs used in our analysis are the ones which have more than 50 members who answered more than 100 questions.

The analysis was performed on a snapshot of the database taken on the 5th of May. The database contains 16,435 users, but not all of them are in a club. Additionally, the database contained 2,451 clubs and 3,902,725 answers to our personality quiz.

The SQL query we used is in the appendix.

tl;dr

The tl;dr is that the results pretty much confirm everything you already knew:

  • /pol/ is one of the lowest boards in empathy whereas /lgbt/ is one of the highest
  • The ted kaczynski connoisseur club scores among the highest in avoidant attachment
  • People who join clubs like loser, femcel, and other self-deprecating terms are pretty high in neuroticism
  • /biz/ scores highest in career focus
  • /fit/ scores highest in fitness focus
  • /out/ and the Catholic club are among the highest in wholesomeness
  • Linux users have a lot of patience
  • /d/ and /gif/ are among the lowest on the "preference for monogamy" metric and among the highest on "sex focus"
  • /sci/ is among the highest in rationality
  • People in the unvaccinated club are very risk-averse, scoring among the lowest on "Thrill-seeking"

Appendix

SQL query to determine trait percentiles

The trait_ratio function is defined here. 

with person_with_traits as (
  SELECT
    person.id,
    person.tiny_id,
    person.name,
    (
      trait_ratio(
        person.presence_score,
        person.absence_score,
        5000
      )
    ).*
  FROM
    person
  where
    count_answers > 100
), averages as (
  select
    trait.name as trait_name,
    club.name as club_name,
    avg(
      person_with_traits.ratio
    )::numeric as result
  from
    person_with_traits
  left join
    person_club
  on
    person_club.person_id =
      person_with_traits.id
  left join
    club
  on
    person_club.club_name =
      club.name
  left join
    trait
  on
    trait.id =
      person_with_traits.trait_id
  where
    trait.name is not null
  and
    club.name is not null
  group by
    trait.name,
    club.name
  having
    count(*) > 50
  order by
    result
)
select
  trait_name,
  club_name,
  round(
    100 * (
      percent_rank()
      over
      (partition by
         trait_name
       order by
         result))::numeric,
    0
  ) as percentile
from
  averages;
