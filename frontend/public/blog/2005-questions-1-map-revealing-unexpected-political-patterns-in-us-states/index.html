I’m sorry, I’ve been living under a rock. Where’s this data from?

Duolicious is a dating app that matches people by personality. Users can answer up to 2005 yes-or-no psychometric questions. (Actually some questions aren’t really psychometric questions. They’re just silly questions.) At the time of writing we’ve got 64,398 registered users in the United States. The data in the interactive chart in this article is aggregated from those users.
