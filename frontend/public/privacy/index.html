Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Data We Collect

Duolicious collects and stores the information you submit about yourself when you sign up or modify your profile. This includes:

  1. Your email address
  2. Your gender
  3. The preferred gender of your partner
  4. Your first name
  5. Your date of birth
  6. Your location (as specified by you)
  7. Your photos
  8. Your self-summary
  9. Your sexual orientation
  10. Your occupation
  11. Your education
  12. Your height
  13. Your preferred relationship duration (e.g. short-term, long-term)
  14. Your smoking status
  15. Your drinking status
  16. Your relationship status
  17. Whether you have children
  18. Whether you want to have children
  19. Your religion
  20. Your ethnicity
  21. Your star sign

Your email address, gender, preferred partner's gender, first name, date of birth, location, and self-summary are required to sign up. Other information is optional.

We also collect and store your Q&A answers.

If you sign up after following a link that carries a referral code (a “ref” parameter in the link), we store that code with your account so we can tell which campaigns and links bring people to Duolicious and pay the partners who share them. We read the code from the link once, when you first open Duolicious, and then remove it from the address bar. It is never stored in cookies or anywhere else on your device.

If you subscribe to Duolicious Gold, we receive details of your subscription from the app store or PayPal, such as the plan, price, payment dates and transaction IDs. We never receive your card details.

We may use cookies to keep you signed in, but we do not use them to track you across the web or to perform analytics.

Data We Do Not Collect

Duolicious does not use cookies to:

  1. Track you on other websites; or
  2. Target ads towards you.

How Your Data Is Used

Duolicious uses the data you provide to:

  1. Create your account
  2. Manage your account
  3. Show your profile to other users
  4. Identify you during requests for support
  5. Recommend you to other users
  6. Recommend other users to you
  7. Understand how users typically use Duolicious so that we can improve our service
  8. Measure which campaigns and links bring new users to Duolicious
  9. Detect and prevent violations of our Terms of Use
  10. Comply with legal requirements

Legal Bases

If you’re in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on these legal bases to use your data:

  1. Contract: to create and run your account, show your profile, recommend people to you, and deliver your messages and subscriptions.
  2. Explicit consent: for the sensitive details you choose to share, such as your sexual orientation, religion, ethnicity and the gender of the people you want to meet, and for verification selfies, except the age check the law requires in the UK. You can withdraw your consent by removing the detail from your profile or, for the gender of the people you want to meet, by deleting your account.
  3. Legitimate interests: to keep Duolicious safe, prevent spam and abuse, improve the service, and measure which campaigns bring people to Duolicious.
  4. Legal obligation: to keep accounting records, respond to lawful requests, and run age checks where the law requires them. In the UK, we use your verification selfie for the required age check under the Data Protection Act 2018’s condition for safeguarding children.

Verification Selfies

Getting verified is optional, except where the law requires an age check before you can chat (currently the United Kingdom).

When you take a verification selfie, we send it to an AI provider (currently OpenAI), together with your profile photos, age, gender and, if you’ve set it, your ethnicity. The AI estimates whether the selfie is an unedited photo of one person doing the requested pose, whether that person is 18 or older and matches your age, gender and ethnicity, and which of your profile photos show the same person. We use those estimates to decide which verification badges you get. A person may also review your selfie, to check the result or to prevent fraud. If you think the result is wrong, you can ask for a person to review it.

We delete your selfie 3 days after you take it, or sooner if you take a new one or delete your account. Under its terms, the provider doesn’t use your selfie to train its models and keeps it for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse. We keep your verification results (which badges you have) until you delete your account.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing support@duolicious.app. We’ll then delete your selfie and your verification results.

How We Share Data

We may disclose your data to comply with a law enforcement investigation or other legal requirements.

We also share data with service providers who help us operate. Depending on the service provider, the shared data may include:

  1. Account data
  2. Profile data
  3. User support data
  4. Subscription and payment data
  5. Verification selfies

At the time of writing, our service providers include Google Cloud Platform, Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare, OpenAI, and the payment processors that handle subscriptions. If you require further details and up-to-date information about the service providers we share your data with, please contact us at support@duolicious.app.

Cross-border Data Transfers

We store your account data in the United States. Our service providers may store your data elsewhere. The sharing of your data with services providers as outlined herein may involve cross-border data transfers to the United States and other countries that might have different laws about data processing. When we transfer data out of the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on adequacy decisions or on the standard contractual clauses in our service providers’ data processing agreements.

Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or restrict your data, to stop using it, or to give you a copy of it, by emailing support@duolicious.app.

If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Deleting Your Account

Deleting your account permanently removes all data associated with your account except for server logs, data stored in the form of backups for the purpose of disaster recovery, and the payment records described below.

If you subscribed after following a link that carries a referral code, we keep a record of each subscription payment: the referral code, plan, amount, currency, date and the payment provider's subscription ID. We keep these records after you delete your account so that we can pay referral partners and meet our accounting obligations.

If your account is banned, it is also deleted. However, we retain the minimum amount of data required to enforce the ban.
